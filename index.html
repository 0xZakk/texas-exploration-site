Building Enduring Businesses in Central Texas

Texas Exploration is a search fund committed to acquiring and stewarding exceptional small businesses. We're looking for established companies with strong teams and solid fundamentals—businesses we can grow for the long term.

Who We Are

Texas Exploration was founded on a simple belief: the best businesses are built on service, endurance, and respect for what came before. We're not looking for quick flips or overnight transformations. We're looking for companies with deep roots, strong cultures, and real potential—businesses we can own and operate for decades.

Based in Austin and focused on Central Texas, we understand the regional landscape and the values that drive successful businesses here. Whether you're a business owner considering succession or a broker representing a quality company, we'd welcome the conversation.

What We're Looking For

Size

$500K–$1.2M in Seller's Discretionary Earnings (SDE) with stable or growing revenues

Location

Central Texas including Austin, San Antonio, and surrounding areas

Industry

Medium-skilled blue collar businesses: manufacturing, distribution, business services, and specialty trades

Characteristics

Established customer base with recurring revenue or repeat clients, strong team in place with potential for continuity

Our Values

Service

We believe businesses exist to serve their employees, customers, and communities. Profit is important, but it's a result of service well-delivered, not an end in itself.

Endurance

We're building for the long term. Our commitment is measured in decades, not quarters. This means making decisions that prioritize sustainable growth over short-term optimization.

Legacy

Every business has a story, a culture, and a legacy built by its founder and team. We see ourselves as stewards of that legacy, honoring what came before while building what comes next.
