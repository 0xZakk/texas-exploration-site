# Texas Exploration — Design System

## Brand Overview

**Texas Exploration** is a search fund dedicated to acquiring and growing a small business in Central Texas. Our brand embodies the values of service, endurance, and legacy.

**Target Audience:**
- Potential investors seeking acquisition opportunities
- Small business brokers in Central Texas
- Small business owners considering exit strategies

---

## Brand Identity

### Mission
To raise capital from investors to acquire and build a lasting small business in Central Texas.

### Tagline
**SERVICE • ENDURANCE • LEGACY**

### Voice & Tone
- **Simple**: Clear, direct language without jargon
- **Confident**: Authoritative without arrogance
- **Professional**: Trustworthy and competent
- **Grounded**: Texas values, practical approach

---

## Visual Design

### Design Principles
1. **Clean & Minimal**: Generous whitespace, uncluttered layouts
2. **Professional**: Credibility-focused, investment-grade presentation
3. **Regional**: Subtle Texas references without cliché
4. **Purposeful**: Every element serves the user journey

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors

**Navy Blue** — Authority & Trust

- RGB: `rgb(4, 44, 108)`
- Usage: Primary headings, main text, key CTAs

**Crimson Red** — Energy & Action
- HEX: `#BF092F`
- RGB: `rgb(191, 9, 47)`
- Usage: Accent elements, secondary CTAs, highlights

### Neutral Colors

**White**
- HEX: `#FFFFFF`
- Usage: Backgrounds, card surfaces

**Off-White**
- HEX: `#F9FAFF`
- RGB: `rgb(249, 250, 255)`
- Usage: Subtle backgrounds, section dividers

**Silver**
- HEX: `#939598`
- RGB: `rgb(147, 149, 152)`
- Usage: Secondary text, borders, subtle elements

**Light Silver**
- HEX: `#EAEAEA`
- RGB: `rgb(234, 234, 234)`

### Color Usage Guidelines

- **Backgrounds**: White (#FFFFFF) or Off-White (#F9FAFF)
- **Primary Text**: Navy Blue (#042C6C)
- **Secondary Text**: Silver (#939598)
- **Links & Buttons**: Navy Blue primary, Crimson Red for emphasis
- **Accents**: Crimson Red sparingly for visual interest
- **Star Icon**: Full-color from logo (Navy + Crimson + White + Silver gradient)

## Typography

### Font Families

**Cooper Hewitt** — Headings
- Modern, geometric sans-serif
- Strong, confident presence
- Used for: H1, H2, H3, navigation, buttons
- Weights: Bold (700), Semibold (600)
- Source: [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cooper+Hewitt) or similar

**Frank Ruhl Libre** — Body Text
- Clean, readable serif
- Professional yet approachable
- Used for: Body copy, paragraphs, lists
- Weights: Regular (400), Medium (500)
- Source: [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Frank+Ruhl+Libre)

### Type Scale

```
H1: 48px / 3rem — Cooper Hewitt Bold — Navy Blue
H2: 36px / 2.25rem — Cooper Hewitt Semibold — Navy Blue
H3: 24px / 1.5rem — Cooper Hewitt Semibold — Navy Blue
H4: 20px / 1.25rem — Cooper Hewitt Semibold — Navy Blue

Body Large: 18px / 1.125rem — Frank Regular — Navy Blue
Body: 16px / 1rem — Frank Regular — Navy Blue
Small: 14px / 0.875rem — Frank Regular — Silver
Caption: 12px / 0.75rem — Frank Regular — Silver
```

### Line Heights
- Headings: 1.2
- Body text: 1.6
- Small text: 1.5

### Letter Spacing
- Headings: -0.02em (slight tightening)
- Body: 0 (default)
- All-caps text: 0.05em (slight expansion)

## Content Guidelines

### Writing Principles
1. **Be Direct**: Lead with the most important information
2. **Be Confident**: State facts without hedging ("We will" not "We hope to")
3. **Be Concise**: One idea per sentence, short paragraphs
4. **Be Specific**: "Central Texas" not "Texas region"

### Example Copy Tone

**Good:**
"We acquire profitable small businesses in Central Texas. Our investors partner with experienced operators to build lasting value."

**Avoid:**
"We're hoping to maybe find some businesses in the Texas area that we might be able to help grow and hopefully create value for everyone involved."
